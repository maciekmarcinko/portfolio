Case studies

TUI.com/flights

Travel · E-commerce · Enterprise

Redesign of the TUI.com booking flow, plus a new Service Package add-on.

Challenge
A short testing window and no benchmarks for presenting service packages.
Approach
Mixed-methods research with unmoderated (Userlytics) and moderated tests, then design iterations.
Result
Shipped, with successful bookings from day one. Built to lift ancillary revenue for thousands of customers a year.
  • Booking flow on desktop, tablet and mobile
  • Booking flow screens
  • Flight search and booking
  • Service package presentation
  • Mobile booking flow
  • Design system documentation: buttons, alerts, icons and form inputs

FaveUp

Own startup · Local business · Mobile + web

My own startup, as CEO and Founder: a simple notification channel that lets local businesses reach their regulars with news and promotions, straight to the phone.

Challenge
Local businesses have loyal customers but no direct way to reach them. Social posts get lost in the algorithm, and an own app is too expensive.
Approach
Problem discovery, a comparison with competitors like Embargo and Stampify, a customer app and an owner panel, and a QR-code test at three local businesses in Brochów.
Result
Almost 200 people signed up to the waitlists during the QR test. The full app is now in development.
  • Customer app: push notification
  • Customer app: news
  • Customer app: places
  • Customer app: settings
  • Owner panel: dashboard
  • Owner panel: create a push
  • Owner panel: QR code
  • Owner panel: settings
